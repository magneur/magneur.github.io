Privacy Policy for Magneur Lifestyle

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Magneur Lifestyle is an iPhone and Mac app provided by Zowlmoxis S.R.L. This policy explains how the app handles workspace content, purchases and optional usage analytics. No separate Magneur account is required.

Your workspace and iCloud

The app saves the content you enter on your device: places and their coordinates, dates, notes, tags and photos; manually entered assets, debts and goals; and tasks, projects, notes and schedules. The app does not connect to bank accounts or obtain payment-card details.

When iCloud is available, the app encrypts workspace records before synchronizing them to your private iCloud database. Encryption keys are stored in Apple Keychain and can synchronize through iCloud Keychain. Apple processes iCloud storage and associated service information under its own policies. Magneur does not operate a separate server for workspace content or receive the plaintext of these encrypted records.

Maps, search and location

The app uses Apple Maps and Apple location services. Map display, place searches and geocoding can send the relevant requests to Apple. If you choose “Center on my location” and grant permission, the app uses your device location to position the map. You can use the map and search without granting permission and can change permission in your device’s Settings. Places you save can include precise coordinates.

Subscriptions, purchases and free trials

Apple processes optional monthly and annual auto-renewing subscriptions and the separate one-time lifetime unlock. Eligible subscription trials are free for the introductory period shown before purchase, then renew at the selected plan’s localized price unless canceled. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account. Existing legacy seven-day non-consumable trials do not renew or charge automatically. The app uses RevenueCat to validate and restore purchases and determine access. RevenueCat receives purchase receipts and transaction information such as product identifiers, purchase dates, prices and refund status, a randomly generated app user identifier, and technical context such as platform, app version and locale. Purchase history is also used for purchase analytics.

The app does not send RevenueCat your workspace content, name, email address or advertising identifier. It does not provide a custom identifier linked to your identity. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy for their processing practices.

Optional usage analytics (PostHog)

If you enable “Share anonymous usage analytics” on the purchase screen, in the Mac workspace sidebar, or in Privacy on iPhone, the app sends limited interaction events to PostHog’s EU ingestion service. Analytics is off by default and the SDK is not initialized until you opt in. You can withdraw consent using the same control; future collection stops. Your choice does not affect access to the app.

Events describe app launches and activations, onboarding steps and completion, purchase-screen views and plan selection, purchase attempts and outcomes, restores, workspace sections opened, and creation of a place, financial item or task. They include an app-specific randomly generated installation identifier, event timestamps, plan or screen identifiers where relevant, purchase error codes, access-confirmation status, and technical metadata such as app version, operating system and device model. The identifier is pseudonymous: it lets us recognize repeat usage on an installation without your name or email. It is not linked to your RevenueCat identifier or Magneur Fit usage.

We do not send task titles, notes, journal text, photos, financial amounts or account details, place names, coordinates, search queries, receipts, advertising identifiers, or Apple account information to PostHog. Session recording, automatic screen capture and automatic interaction capture are disabled. IP addresses are necessarily used to deliver network requests; project IP anonymization is enabled and geographic enrichment is disabled for these events. Lifestyle events are labeled separately from Magneur Fit events within our shared PostHog project.

We use these events to understand onboarding, purchase conversion and feature usage and improve the app. Processing is based on your consent. Events may be queued locally while offline and sent when online while analytics remains enabled. Withdrawing consent does not automatically delete events already received. Contact privacy@magneur.com for privacy requests; without a name or email attached to analytics, we may need your installation identifier to locate records. PostHog retention follows our project’s configured retention and applicable service obligations. See PostHog’s privacy policy for its processing practices and international-transfer information.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not display advertisements or use workspace or purchase information for tracking across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

Workspace records remain locally and, when synchronized, in your private iCloud database until you delete them. Deletions synchronize when a connection is available. Removing the app does not necessarily remove its iCloud data; you can manage iCloud app storage through Apple’s settings. Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase records for purchase restoration, fraud prevention and legal obligations.

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. Avoid sending sensitive workspace content unless necessary. Contact us to ask about information received through support or purchase processing, or to exercise applicable access, correction or deletion rights. Some transaction records may need to be retained by Apple or RevenueCat.

Updates and contact

We will post policy changes here and update the date above. For privacy requests, contact privacy@magneur.com. For app support, contact info@magneur.com.
